Terrain balance

Playground v0.5 · 576 simulated encounters · 12 seeds × two mirrored placements × eight scenarios × three blast radii

Riflemen cost 25 Supply; rocketeers cost 60. Each encounter runs the real fixed-step engine for up to 90 simulation seconds. Green rows show the candidate 40-unit blast, keeping peak damage at 2 HP.

Try a 40-unit blast

At 40, rifles won 7 of 12 open approaches, none of the clustered advances, and all 12 spread advances. Keep the current prices and peak damage; spacing and terrain should matter.

Test method and limits

Damage model: current per-soldier splash; each overlapping soldier receives its own damage. Preassembled ground forces only. No live income, purchase timing, reinforcements, FOBs, capture or aircraft. Timeouts are unresolved, not draws or wins. Mirrored runs are paired checks, not independent seed samples. Survivors are valued at full replacement cost irrespective of remaining HP; raw HP is also retained.

300-Supply forces represent accumulated purchases, not an opening bank. Shared-budget mixed encounters leave Blue 20 Supply unspent; that reserve does not fight or buy reinforcements here. Twelve seeded outcomes provide an initial tuning sample, not proof of universal balance. Paired mirrors test faction symmetry and do not double the independent sample size.

“Blue force” always means the original attacking composition, even when its placement and faction are mirrored. Clustered and spread formations contain the same troops; wider spacing also lengthens the formation and delays rear arrivals.

Economic interpretation

One rocketeer costs 2.4 riflemen. At the current 5 Supply/second income, replacing a rifle consumes 5 seconds of income and replacing a rocketeer consumes 12. These replacement costs guide the comparison; no price, income or main-game weapon changes have been made.
