Does a platoon provide a tactical advantage?

Play isolated encountersPlay campaign
Sometimes. Formation, troop mix and the objective matter.

With equal3R2K forces, Spread wins2/3 primary seeds versus Independent1/3. With rocket-heavy attackers against that same enemy mix, Assault wins3/3 versus Independent0/3. These are specific tests, not universal bonuses.

Against an empty fortified FOB, mixed Siege captures in a median72.0s versus Independent90.8s. Against a staffed FOB, none of the tested five-person attacks captures it, including supplied trials. Survival or lower spending alone is not a successful assault.

The chosen60% retreat/80% resume rule preserves a weakened cohort but can interrupt a winning attack. Leader loss cancels recruitment: a smaller Supply bill can also mean the attack collapsed. There is no hidden damage, health or accuracy buff.

Comparison method and limits

648 actual Campaign-engine runs: nine cases × three attack mixes × four controllers ×3 seeds × two paired faction mirrors. Within a case/mix/seed/color, controllers begin with identical unit types, positions, HP, prices and purchase settings. Leader restrictions, formation, target preferences, retreat and ordinary paid replacement behavior are the tested command policy. Seeded shots may consume randomness differently when tactics change firing events.

Primary counts and medians use the3 blue-side seeds. Mirrored counts are shown separately; the colors are paired robustness checks, not six independent samples. 3 of324 pairs disagree in outcome. Small timing/range-boundary differences can grow into different results; no exact mirrored equality or universal win rate is claimed.

Most cases have fixed forces and no purchases after the identical25-Supply fifth attack rifle. The other four attack soldiers and defenders are explicit preassembled scenario grants. Initial full replacement values include that rifle once; additional Supply excludes its25 to avoid double counting. Supplied FOB starts both accounts at120, attacker95 after that shared rifle transaction, then earns normal5/s, capped240, with25/60 HQ replacements,2s spacing and15 living cap. Independent troops replace the same lost roster types; platoons do so while their leader survives. This isolates ordinary paid replenishment rather than running the normal campaign commander. Defenders have fortification and protected posts whose tactical value is not priced as infantry.

All controllers share the improved rule that rifles wait outside intact enemy defenses for a breach; a separately labeled Legacy Independent option in the lab preserves old walk-past behavior and is excluded from this main matrix. All three platoon mixes can now capture an empty outpost: an initial rocket-heavy median/cohesion stall was found, fixed and regression-tested before this final matrix.

Survivor value is full replacement price at each run’s own stopping time, not a valuation of remaining HP. Capture median uses successful captures only. Unfinished means no settled win/loss by that case’s limit, not a draw or proof of impossibility. All eliminations wait for in-flight projectiles. No aircraft, natural campaign win claim, free reinforcements, post-start injected damage or forced wins. Only three seeds and a flat battlefield; terrain and human tactics can change conclusions.
