# First sorties: JSON battlefield campaign

Open `campaign.html` from the Battlefield main menu. `campaign.json` orders the level IDs/files. Append a validated map and a catalog entry to add a future mission. First assault is initially available; capturing its red HQ unlocks Ridgeline. Victory unlocks the next level, defeat/draw do not. Retry or next level creates a completely fresh world. Completed IDs alone persist in a versioned local key; blocked storage retains session unlocks with an honest warning.

Each battlefield uses `schemaVersion:1`, `kind:battlefield`, id/name/description/seed, `world:{width,baseline}`, piecewise-linear terrain `[x,elevation]`, stable-ID bases, helipad, both faction deployments/opening orders/wave composition, `balanceProfile:campaign-v1` and `objective:{type:capture-hq,target:red-hq}`. Terrain accepts the same supported heightfield bounds as the lab. Bases require flat170-unit footprints, pads flat200, with no overlapping structures. Each faction has exactly one HQ. Catalog filenames must match their safe level ID. Definitions validate/clone before replacing state; unsupported profiles/versions or failed fetches leave a stopped recoverable menu.

Campaign-v1 deliberately retains fair starting accounts120, income5/s, bank240,25/60 infantry prices,2s purchases,15 living cap and3 rifles+2 rockets per side. Blue purchases are manual; red uses its paid commander. The first free helicopter has100 hull/120 ammo. Paid90 replacement,10s minimum wait, parked5HP/s repair and30round/s rearm remain. Shared40-radius exposed-infantry splash and height-dependent ground rocket trajectories come from the common lab modules. Bunker protection, helicopter damage and structural breach stay separate rules; FOB20/HQ6 valid rocket hits, physical rifle5s capture. The current skirmish remains separately playable with its accepted legacy combat profile.

Campaign scene/rendering adapt the pure `campaign/world.js`; no DOM/storage/live clock in that world. `combat/groundInfantry.js` owns shared terrain target selection, rifle muzzle/bearing and rocket weapon state. Lab and campaign both call it, with the same geometry/trajectory/random-share primitives. Campaign adds occupation, orders, paid commanders, anti-air, buildings and pilot lifecycle. A conservative physical precheck and exact-input memoization avoid repeatedly solving impossible/unchanged rocket trajectories; validated level geometry is immutable. Different world scheduling/final ground-contact substeps may produce small numerical differences; cross-scene encounter probes and full regressions check practical consistency.

First assault configures a2800-unit flat map with red2rifle+1rocket field waves. Ridgeline configures3600 units, a125-unit ridge, an elevated enemy FOB and3rifle+2rocket waves. Neither changes weapon statistics or faction income. Buildings/pad render at their real terrain elevation; helicopter/rifle bullets and rockets collide with hills, and the aircraft follows physical terrain contact.

Reproduce the final support study with `CAMPAIGN_SEEDS=3 CAMPAIGN_SECONDS=600 npm run test:campaign`. It applies an explicit benchmark-only blue purchasing policy and actual flight/fire/turn controls. This automation is never installed as player-side AI in the game. Each case records real captures, ammo, repair departures, replacement generation, spending, troops and base state. `node scripts/render-campaign-results.mjs` renders the readable comparison. Initial420-second/controller experiments are archived separately and are not final difficulty evidence. See `campaign-results.html` for final outcomes and bounded interpretation; additional seeds/manual mobile play remain valuable.

Test-only button/controller helpers are documented in `quality/control-testing.md` and are attached only under`?test`. Normal campaign URLs offer no progression bypass or testing controller.
