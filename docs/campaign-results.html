How the comparison works

The same explicit blue purchase policy maintains riflemen and rocketeers using ordinary25/60 Supply transactions,2s deployment spacing and the15-unit cap. Red uses its real paid commander. No support leaves the helicopter parked; limited support allows up to40 rounds; sustained support flies actual control inputs, reacts to visible missiles and returns to the pad for repair/rearm. No teleporting, injected damage, free purchases or forced victories are used.

“Unfinished” means neither headquarters was captured by 600 simulation seconds. It is neither a draw nor proof the map cannot be won. Different maps use their configured seeds. Three samples and one purchase/pilot policy demonstrate a support/logistics difference, not universal balance or a mathematical requirement that every ground-only strategy must fail. A human may find better tactics. No level timer is imposed in the game.

Only completed mission IDs are saved on this device. Each attempt gets fresh troops, Supply, hull and ammunition. Helicopters clear troops; rockets breach defenses; riflemen capture headquarters.
