# PL-07–10: why cohesion and retreat, and what the controlled trials show

The purpose is command discipline, not free combat power. A leader500 units behind his screen cannot remain visibly part of its fight. The revised compact slots stay within220 of the leader, captures have a280-unit leash, and leader goals remain within240 of the median physically joined cohort. Actual positions correct at ordinary speed: a casualty can shift the median instantly, but nobody teleports. Incoming HQ soldiers are excluded until they physically join. The first80-unit goal band stalled rocket-heavy platoons because the expected rear-weighted median itself exceeded that band. That defect was reproduced against an empty FOB, initially widened to160; an exhaustive survivor-mask audit then found the same issue after a rifle casualty. The final240 band strictly exceeds the220 maximum role offset and was tested across all supported above-threshold survivor masks before the final matrix. Original controlled rows are archived separately as cohesion-initial-controlled-results.json.

The user selected60% combat-ready retreat and80% recovery, with at least6s of physical regroup. A five-person squad retreats at three combat-ready soldiers and can resume at four, avoiding repeated oscillation around one threshold. The group chooses a fixed point320 units rearward or safe friendly cover behind within500, bounded by its HQ rally. Compact posts are distinct and larger rosters fit within72 of the regroup anchor. Rocket soldiers break ground aim while moving back; valid priority AA can still stop them, and rifles retain ordinary slow firing-walk. Withdrawal suppresses capture. There is no free repair, armor, damage, fire-rate or retreat-speed bonus. Paid replacements travel from HQ at2× until joining; the normal costs/cooldown/cap remain. Loss of the leader ends automatic platoon replenishment.

The intended advantages are preserving expensive rockets, keeping rifle support close enough to exploit a breach, and making a deliberate second attack instead of feeding replacements into a dispersed force. The costs are loss of ground, delay, reduced fire during withdrawal, and a vulnerable25-Supply leader who consumes one rifle slot. A retreat is not a victory. An isolated supplied attack ends on complete attacking-wave elimination; it does not buy a fresh leader/new wave or claim the campaign headquarters was defeated. A low spending total can reflect leader death and recruitment cancellation rather than efficient fighting.

## Controlled comparison

`npm run test:tactics` executes648 actual-engine runs: nine JSON cases × three five-person attack mixes × four controllers × three seeds (101–103) × two paired faction mirrors. The public laboratory uses exactly the same encounter module and catalog. Attack mixes are3R2K (195),4R1K (160),2R3K (230); opponents include three rifles (75),2R1K (110),3R2K (195),seven rifles (175),5R2K (245) and2R3K (230). Numerical equality is not automatically equal Supply value; all values are recorded. Defenders also have the physical advantage of a fortified FOB in those cases, not an assumed infantry-equivalent structure price.

For each scenario/mix/seed/color, every controller starts with the same types, HP, world positions and account rules. Initial troops are explicitly preassembled scenario grants; every controller pays25 for its equivalent fifth attack rifle, leaving95 from120. Initial full Supply value includes this rifle; additional spending excludes its25. These fixtures are not natural campaign deployments. Most cases keep a fixed roster with no income/replacements. Supplied FOB enables common5/s income/240bank/25–60 prices/2s spacing/15cap and ordinary HQ replacements for both sides, using the same primitives. Independent troops replace lost roster types; sergeant recruitment ends if the leader dies.

Independent control uses ordinary advancing/fire/targeting with the new shared rifle rule: wait outside intact fortifications until rockets breach. Platoons use the same rule plus role slots, cohesion, doctrine targeting, withdrawal and resupply discipline. A separate legacy-independent lab option retains the previous walking-past-an-intact-base behavior; it is excluded from the main matrix. Siege keeps rifles outside bunker rifle reach during bombardment, then closes after breach. Protected enemy launchers can still kill its exposed screen. All weapons, trajectories, shared blasts, bunker protection,20-point FOB defense and5s capture remain the real engine's rules. No aircraft or normal campaign AI policy runs in these controlled fixtures.

## Interpretation

The generated mobile report is the numerical source for each case; raw rows preserve HP, costs, stop times, breach/capture, retreat/regroup/resume and projectile counts. Primary summaries use three blue-side seeds. Mirrored outcomes are displayed separately as paired robustness checks, not extra independent samples; some near-boundary timings produce different outcomes, and the report states the exact discordance count. Unfinished is not a draw. Survivor Supply is full replacement price at each run's own stopping time, not current HP value. All elimination outcomes wait for in-flight projectiles.

Specific current observations: equal mixed forces favor Spread in these samples (2/3 primary wins versus independent1/3); rocket-heavy attackers against equal mixed opposition favor Assault (3/3 versus independent0/3). Neither establishes general superiority. Mixed Siege captures empty FOBs sooner (median72.0s versus independent90.8s), rocket-heavy Assault/Siege also capture it sooner (medians62.8/61.5s versus independent69.1s), while Spread is slower at73.9s. Staffed FOBs are not captured by any tested five-person mix/controller, with or without paid replacements. A defended base remains a strong combined-arms challenge; this study does not prove that every ground strategy fails or that helicopter support is mathematically necessary.

The60% withdrawal policy can delay an already winning fight: against the smaller2R1K force, independent mixed attackers finish in median16.4s while mixed platoons take roughly65–76s, with the same median120 surviving Supply value. This is a real disadvantage, not an advantage to hide. The first refinement therefore provides a coherent, observable grouping/retreat behavior and identifies where it helps or hurts; it is not claimed as a complete universal balance solution. Three seeds, one flat layout and these controllers are a bounded experiment. Battlefield terrain and human decisions need further playtesting.

Old v2.1 campaign timing studies include different purchasing schedules and are historical whole-policy evidence. They are not relabeled as this controlled formation comparison. All new behavior is wired into both existing Campaign maps through the shared unit/controller modules; the lab is a separate place to inspect it. Classic skirmish and Flight School keep their accepted mechanics. The branch remains unmerged until user acceptance. Physical iPhone Safari remains a separate review gate.
