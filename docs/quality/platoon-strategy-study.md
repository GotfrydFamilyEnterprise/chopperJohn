# PL-01–06: paid sergeant formations, v2.1

The Campaign deployment chevron buys a25-Supply rifle sergeant and authorizes that platoon's automatic recruitment and replacement. Initial assembly may reuse eligible free opening/advance soldiers near rally; guards and garrison reservations stay at their posts. Every casualty/detachment, including during rally, queues a new same-type paid HQ soldier. No funds or population room means waiting, with no charge. Replacements travel physically at twice normal surface speed while out of combat until joined; weapons, health and firing cadence remain normal. Losing the leader ends resupply and releases surviving paid members.

The JSON wave includes the leader in its rifle count and requires2+ rifles and1+ rocket, at most10 soldiers. First assault blue uses3R2K; red retains2R1K. Ridgeline uses3R2K on both sides. Maximum two leaders and15 total living soldiers per faction, normal2s deployment spacing,25/60 costs and existing independent Supply accounts. Red garrison/emergency/AA orders retain first claim on purchases and saving. Blue's regular infantry remains manual unless a sergeant has been explicitly deployed.

Three spatial doctrines use the same weapons and accounts:

- Assault: rifle screen210/170 units from an intact base, rockets490/450 away, leader650 away. Engages rockets within550.
- Siege: rifles300/265 units away, rockets620/570 away, leader800 away. Prefers intact defenses when a real trajectory is clear, engaging within700 subject to the existing height-dependent ballistic reach. This is the initial UI selection after the comparisons below.
- Spread: wider rifle/rocket intervals, leader680 away, rocket engagement within550. Spreading reduces clustering but can make the screen less reliable. It does not promise immunity from area damage.

Those distances describe an unobstructed settled formation relative to an intact base. Terrain, enemy contact, movement and physical replacements change positions; riflemen leave formation to capture a breached base under the existing rules. The leader stays back. Siege does not impose a new700-unit weapon maximum or remove the ordinary minimum range. No special statistical damage/accuracy/HP bonus is attached to any doctrine.

## Experiment

`npm run test:platoons` runs the real Campaign engine at120Hz using both JSON maps and three adjacent fixed seeds: First assault31/32/33, Ridgeline73/74/75. Four explicit blue purchase policies face the same red Assault-platoon commander. Independent blue uses the existing adaptive manual purchase policy; the other cases explicitly deploy up to two sergeants and let their paid rosters refill. These are complete command policies, not equal unit lists: composition, arrival timing, expenditures and casualties differ and are recorded. Manual/pilot benchmark controllers are never installed as blue AI in the game.

The24 ground-only runs stop at480 simulated seconds. Raw final evidence is `evidence/platoon-strategy-results.json`. No headquarters fell:24 unfinished, zero wins/losses. Unfinished does not mean a draw or proof that a strategy cannot win. Siege and independent blue each captured the enemy FOB in6/6 samples; Assault1/6; Spread0/6. Siege's First assault capture times were157.6/285.8/202.3s (median202.3), versus independent279.6/269.6/273.6s (median273.6). Siege was faster in two of three samples, not universally faster. On Ridgeline, independent captured sooner in two of three paired seeds:235.3/263.3/235.3s versus Siege416.2/191.6/307.1s. Siege's median307.1s is slower than independent235.3s. Capturing an outpost does not guarantee holding it or winning HQ; raw traces include recaptures.

A separate eight-run supplement uses the existing sustained helicopter control policy, one seed per map and a600s cutoff (`PLATOON_AIR=active PLATOON_SEEDS=1 PLATOON_SECONDS=600`). Actual controls, shots, ammo, repairs/rearm, red AA, incoming evasion and paid aircraft replacement remain live. Raw evidence is `evidence/platoon-air-results.json`. Siege won First assault at123.3s/150 helicopter rounds vs independent137.7s/149; Siege won Ridgeline at145.7s/195 rounds vs independent301.9s/352. Assault won First assault132.0s but remained unfinished on Ridgeline; Spread remained unfinished on First assault and won Ridgeline145.0s/221 rounds. This is an exploratory one-seed pilot result, not a reliable win probability or universal balance conclusion. It supports trying a coordinated Siege advance with helicopter cover; it does not justify buffing every unit or making every doctrine equivalent.

All runs include the point-blank rifle collision correction: the first collision sweep includes the barrel segment, preventing opposing riflemen sharing a guard/capture post from becoming immune. FOB defenses remain20 rocket hits, HQ6, rifle capture5s, and accepted cover/shared-blast rules remain. This real bug fix is tested independently from doctrine comparisons.

Initial failed forward-command trials are preserved as `evidence/platoon-initial-formation.json`; a later rear-command diagnostic is `evidence/platoon-rear-command-probe.json`. They exposed excessive leader attrition and led to moving the leader behind the rifle screen and rockets. They are diagnostic historical trials, not mixed into final sample counts.

## Scope and evidence

Grouping is isolated in `js/battlefield/ai/platoons.js`, with Campaign composition-root wiring, renderer and button/dropdown changes. Classic skirmish and Flight School retain their accepted gameplay. Both existing campaign maps load the shared grouping/infantry rules; no separate duplicate unit simulation is introduced. The full regression suite, new lifecycle/cap/accounting tests, real browser button tests, mobile screenshots and independent review are recorded in implementation-status and `platoon-adversarial-review.md`. Physical iPhone Safari remains a manual review gate. Preview publication is separate from merging the branch into production.
