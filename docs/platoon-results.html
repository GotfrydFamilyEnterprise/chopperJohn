What these numbers do—and do not—show

Ground:24 runs, two JSON maps, three fixed seeds and four blue command policies. Same red Assault-platoon controller, normal prices/income/cooldown/population cap. Independent blue uses adaptive explicit manual purchases. Other policies buy up to two sergeants and their normal-price rosters refill automatically. Composition, timing, casualties and spending differ: this compares complete command policies, not identical unit lists.

Air supplement:8 runs, one fixed seed per map, existing sustained pilot policy with actual controls, ammo, repair/rearm, AA and paid replacement. It explores helicopter cooperation; one seed cannot establish win probability or universal superiority.

Outpost counts mean captured at least once, not held at cutoff. Median FOB times include only successful captures. Unfinished means no HQ result at the cutoff, neither a draw nor proof of impossibility. No teleporting, free purchases, injected damage or forced natural-play victories. Both matrices include the point-blank bullet collision correction. FOB defenses20, HQ6 and capture5s remain unchanged.

Initial forward-command trials lost leaders too readily; final tactics place the leader behind the screen. Physical iPhone Safari still needs user review. These benchmark controllers never run as blue AI in the game.
